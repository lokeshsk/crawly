## Literature Review Synthesis: Deep Learning & Foundation Architectures

### 1. Conceptual Progression & Paradigms
The literature demonstrates a fundamental transition from recurrent and convolutional architectures (He et al., 2016) toward universal attention-based Transformer backbones (Vaswani et al., 2017). Initially, deep residual networks established that identity mappings resolve optimization degradation in high-capacity models. Subsequently, Vaswani et al. removed temporal recurrence entirely in favor of multi-head dot-product attention.

### 2. Pre-training and Scale Dynamics
Devlin et al. (2019) demonstrated that bidirectional context modeling via masked objectives (BERT) vastly outperforms left-to-right baselines on classification and extraction tasks. Brown et al. (2020) further expanded scale frontiers to 175B parameters, establishing that in-context learning emerges naturally without gradient fine-tuning.

## Automated Research Gaps & Open Challenges Roadmap

### Gap 1: Quadratic Complexity vs. Infinite Context Windows
- **Bottleneck Identified:** The foundational self-attention formulation (Vaswani et al., 2017) scales quadratically O(N^2) with context length.
- **Current Solutions:** Linear attention approximations, FlashAttention tiled kernel optimizations, and state-space models (Mamba/S4).
- **Open Challenge:** Preserving needle-in-a-haystack associative recall across context lengths exceeding 10M tokens without degradation.

### Gap 2: Factual Hallucinations & Verifiable Attribution
- **Bottleneck Identified:** Autoregressive scaling (Brown et al., 2020) generates convincing syntactic text without strict semantic veracity guarantees.
- **Open Challenge:** Developing citation-grounded RAG architectures with deterministic citation provenance and automated contradiction detection.

### Gap 3: Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning (PEFT) Under Extreme Scale
- **Bottleneck Identified:** Full parameter fine-tuning of 100B+ models is economically intractable for localized research domains.
- **Open Challenge:** Adapting localized low-rank adapters (LoRA/QLoRA) with zero catastrophic forgetting of pre-trained world knowledge.
